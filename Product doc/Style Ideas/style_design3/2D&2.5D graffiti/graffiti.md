## 2D/2.5D Graffiti

---
Basically we could add shadow coating to achieve a 2D or 2.5D effect, 
the visual effect will be very cute and natural, which is in line with our initial 
idea of the penguin image in our own game. We found inspiration from cartoon 
animation *Bluey, Pokemon, ultimate chicken horse* and *goose goose duck*.

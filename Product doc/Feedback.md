We've received some feedback from other groups，which inspired us on how to improve our project：

<h3>What they like</h3>

- zoom in & zoom out;
- backgrounds generate as you go;

<h3>What makes them confused</h3> 

- Under what circumstances will the game end？
- how could players make the penguins go faster?

<h3>What could be improved</h3>

- adding cracks between the glaciers where the penguins would actually fall would make the game more difficult; 
- zoom out is too wide; 
- a maximum cap on the gravity value could be devised; 
- The game should become progressively more difficult as it progresses; 
- adding obstacles both in the sky and on the ground(e.g. Add wind blowing as one of the hindrances when the penguin is flying through the air)

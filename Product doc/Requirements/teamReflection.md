## Epics, User stories and Acceptance Criteria

Building an app is not an easy task. Getting ideas together to create what will be the 
final product has to take in consideration what really matters to the users. The introduction 
of **Epics**, **User Stories** and **Acceptance Criteria** during this activity was key to 
understanding how to build a map that will help us keep track of our final destination; a 
well-developed application.

We started this journey brainstorming any idea we got in mind. When we got a pool of them, we noticed 
that it is really easy to get lost in all the features we want to add (and according to our different 
personal criteria). The introduction of **Epics** as the big main feature that can be divided into 
specific tasks (**stories**) proved to be an excellent way to stay organised with clear goals.
During this activity, we noticed how easy it is to keep everyone on the same track by effectively
communicating our ideas in the group when following this structure, and at the same time, aiming for
a well and organised app development.

We also learned how important is to define when a task is done and works correctly.
Peeking at the future of this project, listing a clear **Acceptance Criteria** will be key to
notice when the implemented features will meet the group expectations.

As we said at the beginning of this reflection, building an app with a team is not an easy task. 
We believe that this introduction to **Agile development** has shown us that this is a strong 
"philosophy" of how to work in a team effectively, and we are more than excited to keep learning 
about it while using it during our game project.
